# Blog editorial covers — November 2026

38 article-specific covers: 26 news stories and 12 releases, shared across 13 locales.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool using the approved `evox-news-editor` skill. Five covers reuse the approved reference artwork for their original subjects. The other 33 were generated individually from each article’s English body and scene brief. All illustrations are editorial concepts; original research figures and product screenshots remain intact.

## Art direction

Neutral white and gray surfaces, charcoal contours, sparse crimson focal details, and localized print texture. Concrete scenes vary with article content. Avoid pink casts, large red fills, copied UI, invented metrics, and generated equations. Covers are optimized WebP files; article bodies, dates, routes, and page templates are unchanged apart from cover references and localized alternative text.
