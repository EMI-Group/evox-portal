**EvoX v1.4.0** is now available! This release is primarily a maintenance update focused on bug fixes, compatibility improvements, and general polish across the framework — including support for newer PyTorch versions, refinements to evolutionary-strategy variants and the Triton kernel infrastructure, cleaner algorithm import/export chains, documentation updates, and a stronger test suite.

If you've wondered what kept us busy over the past 10 months: we have been building [**`EvoX Genesis`**](https://github.com/EMI-Group/genesis), an AI agent system that can reliably navigate, reason over, modify and build massive codebases (100k+ LOC). Genesis is actively powering the next chapter of EvoX, enabling us to accelerate development at an unprecedented speed and scale. You can track our first major fruits of this workflow landing soon via our `etl-refactor` branch — stay tuned! 🚀
