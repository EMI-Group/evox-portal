# src/components/

## Intent
Shared site chrome, decorative SVG components, and page-body components for the Astro 7 EvoX Portal. Layout renders Header, main content, and Footer as siblings. HomeContent uses `<Layout fullBleed>` to retain shared chrome while omitting the main element's header padding. Its opening hands off to the shared upper-left header brand. RuntimeContent and EcosystemContent use standard padded Layout content.

## API Surface
- **BrandMark.astro** — `size?: number` (default 29px). Server-rendered SVG port of liangfang-wen's public Genesis commit `bbb8c45`, retaining all 18 paths, 23ms sweep steps, 150ms easing, and permanent red diagonal. Used directly in Footer and Explore, and inside BrandWordmark for Header, the homepage opening, and hero. Colors respond to `html[data-theme]`; reduced motion disables transitions. Decorative SVGs rely on the parent element's accessible name.
- **BrandWordmark.astro** — No props. Combines the supplied `新版本evox-svg.zip` vector EVO lettering, its original gradient, and source proportions with the existing animated BrandMark X. The source is preserved in `../assets/brand/evox-wordmark-source.svg`. The responsive wrapper fills its parent's width; each instance generates a unique gradient ID. Theme colors adapt through SVG stops and the X's existing per-path sweep. The opening, hero, and header share identical vector geometry for a precise flying-logo handoff. The wrapper and SVG are decorative, with accessible naming supplied by their parents.
- **Header.astro** — `locale?: Locale`, `localePath?: string`; legacy `animated?` and `sticky?` props remain declared but have no behavior. The header is fixed and 64px tall. Its `.header-glass` layer is a full-width glass bar without JavaScript. With JavaScript it is transparent at the page top (`data-at-top`) and, once scrolled, morphs into a floating Liquid Glass capsule (`data-floating`) that matches the 1216px inner width. Dropdowns and the mobile panel are denser Liquid Glass cards. The brand remains on the left, followed by navigation and actions in matching DOM and keyboard order. The full wordmark wrapper exposes `[data-intro-logo]` as the opening's flight destination. Header is immediately visible on other routes; HomeIntro controls its visibility during the homepage opening without changing its measured position. Navigation starts with the localized Home link targeting the localized homepage URL without a hash fragment, followed by Ecosystem, Blog (News/Releases), Docs (Guide/Tutorials/API/Runtime), GitHub, an outlined Contact Us button, and all 13 languages. Contact Us opens `https://chengran.tech/` in a new tab and appears as a full-width button at the bottom of the mobile menu. Desktop dropdowns use native `<details>` with hover, click, ArrowDown, outside-click, and Escape handling. Up to 1023px wide, a `<details>` mobile menu replaces desktop navigation. Locale links preserve query/hash. Theme buttons use translated `theme.toggle`, sync `aria-pressed`, persist `evox-theme`, and respond to storage events.
- **HomeIntro.astro** — `scrollLabel: string`, supplied from translated `hero.scroll`. A fixed full-viewport overlay shows the standalone BrandMark X on an initial top-of-page visit, with a cue linking to `#home`. One downward wheel gesture, click/tap anywhere, upward one-finger swipe, or PageDown/ArrowDown/Space/End press starts a 1100ms native Web Animations flight: the standalone X translates and scales to the upper-left `[data-intro-logo] .wordmark-x`, while the curtain and cue fade. Source and destination are measured once and the native scrollbar stays in place. The target remains hidden until handoff. States are `active`, `entering`, and permanently `complete` for the current document; completion hides the overlay without leaving scrollable space. Underlying homepage sections and chrome are temporarily inert; input listeners use an AbortController and are removed on completion. Keyboard entry focuses the hero title. Escape, restored scroll, non-top hash changes, interruption handlers, and a 1.6-second watchdog finish safely. Deep links and restored positions skip the overlay, and returning to `#top` cannot replay it. Reduced motion enters instantly. Without JavaScript, the overlay stays hidden and the normal homepage remains usable. A four-second module-load fallback also releases the curtain if the component bundle fails.
- **EvolutionField.astro** — `generationLabel: string` (translated `scene.generation`), `quietSelector?: string`, `coverSelector?: string` (elements drawn over the field, the homepage news cards). Decorative full-width Canvas behind the homepage hero: a live evolutionary algorithm on a four-peak fitness landscape drawn as marching-squares contours. Height reads two ways: contour opacity rises with level (every fifth is an index contour), and a grid-resolution accent tint grows with height alone (no text-aware factor, which would print fixed rectangles into it), so the taller of two peaks is redder; it stays faint (alpha follows height to the power 2.2) and mostly marks the summits. The landscape follows the Moving Peaks benchmark: it holds still for 10s so the population visibly converges, then shifts over 2.4s (contour strength rises by at most 25%). Every landscape, the first included, is generated anew: four broad, overlapping peaks of varied size, elongation and angle, so contours (20 levels, 0.08 apart) fill the page; it is sampled many times and the most spread-out layout kept. The tallest peak starts at a side of the field, and the summit (found on a coarse grid, since peaks overlap) stays clear of quiet text and cover elements and away from the previous one; on narrow screens this relaxes step by step. The optimum reticle marks the highest grid node, cursor peak included. On a shift each old peak travels to the new peak that keeps the total movement shortest. Each 820ms generation applies fitness sharing to form niches, keeps one elite per niche (red dots the size of ordinary agents), and breeds the rest by tournament selection and Gaussian mutation with about 10% random immigrants. Offspring animate out from their parents along fading lineage lines. A rotating reticle marks the current optimum, and only the overall best individual adds a halo, ring and a plot-style annotation (an elbow leader and bare text, no background) showing just the localized generation (no fitness value); it keeps its last corner unless that would leave the field or cover page text, then tries the other three. Nearby best-marker changes ease over 360ms; changes to another niche fade out and back in over 420ms, keeping the halo, dot, ring and generation annotation together. Population positions and historical trails remain exact; reduced motion shows the current marker immediately. The best marker and annotation keep an opacity floor so they stay legible inside the quiet zone around the hero copy. A solid red line traces the best individual's per-generation moves (at most 24 points), jumps to another niche (including the cursor-held peak) included; each new segment grows out from the previous point over 360ms, and every point fades with age over 7s, the opacity carried along each segment by a gradient so the trail recedes smoothly from its oldest end. A landscape shift still fades the whole trail out over 1.2s. On desktop, while a mouse cursor rests in the upper 70% of the hero, it raises the ground beneath it to just above the tallest landscape peak (over an existing peak it only tops it up, with a small floor), marked by a halo and a dashed ring; the population migrates to it, and the peak fades once the cursor leaves. Individuals and lines fade near elements matching `quietSelector` (hero copy and header text). It pauses offscreen or when the document is hidden; reduced motion evolves offline and renders one static generation. Keep the per-frame cost flat: the landscape grid is cached and resampled only during shifts, the cursor bump is added only within 3.2 sigma, contours are rebuilt only when either changes, and text fading reads a quiet map precomputed on resize. Population and trails are bounded. A fixed-size offscreen canvas caches the landscape between changes; the hero and its cache are each capped at three million pixels. Rendering is capped at about 30fps, with cursor resampling limited by elapsed time rather than display refresh rate.
- **Footer.astro** — `locale?: Locale`. Compact footer with the shared vector BrandWordmark at 100px wide, resource links, five social links, and translated copyright. The standalone collaboration section has been removed; Contact Us now lives in Header. On mobile, resource links use a three-column grid with 44px minimum touch targets; social links also have 44px targets, with compact spacing and left-aligned branding/copyright. Community targets `/libs#community` in the active locale. An inline script refreshes the copyright year.
- **EvolutionDiagram.astro** — Canvas animation adapted from the supplied evolution-ring HTML. Parameter-signature tiles represent candidates without tying them to an application. Three phases show generation, relative evaluation bars, and three retained signatures. After selection, the parents persist into the next Generate phase: offspring copy their traits, changed parameter segments are highlighted, and the new candidates spread into the population. Localized phase titles and captions explain each stage. The outer three-stage ring remains fixed, with reduced decorative guides. Props provide localized step names, description, pause/play, and restart labels. It supports stage selection, reduced motion, resize, and offscreen suspension. HomeContent places explanatory text beside it on desktop and above it on mobile. Its deterministic generation cache retains at most three generations; backward seeks outside that window replay the seeded evolution. Rendering is capped at about 30fps. The inline script names its class `EvolutionDiagram` after the component and exposes the class as `window.EvolutionDiagram` and the mounted instance as `window.evolutionDiagram`.
- **ParadigmArt.astro** — Accepts `kind` (`ParadigmKind`) and `locale`, and renders `art/ParadigmScene.astro` directly inside the shared `.paradigm-surface` frame. Four distinct Canvas scenes share the evolution diagram’s theme-aware surface, thin lines, neutral palette and restrained red halos. `art/ParadigmScene.astro` owns localized pause/restart controls and one shared pulsing-dots loading state (static under reduced motion) shown until the canvas is ready; `art/paradigm-scenes.ts` (`ParadigmScene`, mounted by `mountParadigmScenes()`; `PopulationKind` covers the population-based Generative, Physical and Science scenes) owns rendering, responsive sizing and playback on a shared 14.6-second cycle. Do not add the parent evolution diagram’s five-step captions or an explicit feedback-loop graphic to these cards. Each scene has one localized phase caption beneath the canvas. All results are illustrative; no performance metrics are claimed.
  - **Generative (title “Foundation Models”)** shows three competing model matrices and the retained model on a 14.6-second loop: the current generation, a persistent retained model, and the next generation. Candidates are evaluated with illustrative relative objective bars; one exact elite is retained, each of two offspring mutates at most three weights (only those mutations are red), and the actual offspring move into the next evaluation cycle.
  - **Agentic (Genesis)** treats Genesis as a recursive-agent (RLM-style) framework and draws one episode as a 3D git graph in an oblique projection (no perspective, so text stays level): time runs left to right, each recursion depth is a translucent floor (localized labels: root agent, subagents, nested subagents), and siblings recede across a floor. `art/recursive-agents.ts` defines the episode as a binary tree of seven agents; task names (`compiler`, `parser`, `lexer`…) are code literals that rotate over three episodes. The present is one vertical time plane through all floors (a tick on `main`); floors and lane text exist only to its left and are revealed as it moves, while `main` is always drawn in full, and the plane fades at the end. Every agent branch follows one rule: a node marks where it starts; the line after a node is dashed (red while the agent works, muted while it waits) until the next node lands, then that stretch sweeps solid from left to right and the node appears as the sweep arrives. Every node is a commit: filled on `main`, hollow on agent branches (the root's included). Branch starts, the parent's fork point and earlier commits are small and unlabelled; the branch's final commit or merge is full size with its 7-character id just above the lane to the left; each pops with a faint halo pulse. The root's first node sits directly below `main`'s base (the same commit), and its merge directly below `main`'s new commit. Agents commit with different rhythms that rotate per episode (`RHYTHMS` in `art/recursive-agents.ts`): small frequent commits, one large commit, or a long gap; the front parent commits its own work while it waits, and the back parent commits a plan before it dispatches. Earlier commits are timed to stay clear of every id label. Dispatch and merge share one hand-off: a dashed guide grows in a straight line behind a red dot, then fades, leaving no permanent edges between floors. A parent dispatches all its subagents at once, and each parent picks its own moment, so nested subagents start at different times; once every subagent has finished, the surviving subagents' guides rise together and arrive with the parent's own sweep at its merge commit. `main` then fast-forwards to the root's merge: a guide rises straight up and the same commit (same time, same id) appears on `main`; as the episode fades out it slides back to the base position, so each episode visibly continues the last. On alternating episodes one nested commit is rejected: its branch stops at a red node with a struck-out id and ×, is not merged, and then fades with its lane into a muted fossil that stays in the history.
  - **Physical** compares three four-parameter controllers (stride, lift, balance, timing) driving identical robots in separate lanes; **Science** tests airfoil variants (camber, thickness, camber location) in a stylized flow field. Both retain one exact elite and mutate one parameter in each of two offspring, marking only changes red; neither claims physics or CFD results.
  - Canvas pauses offscreen and when the document is hidden; reduced motion shows a static resolved scene with optional manual playback. Legacy `art/genesisTree.ts`, `agenticLifecycle.ts` and `agenticPlayer.ts` remain on disk but are not mounted.
- **pages/HomeContent.astro** — Homepage with a compact text hero and latest-update cards immediately below, Layout's shared chrome, native section anchors, optional scroll reveals, and chapter rail at widths of at least 1280px. The localized intro overlay is mounted before the unchanged hero; curtain fade and staggered content reveals accompany its logo flight. See `pages/CONTEXT.md`.
- **pages/EcosystemContent.astro** — Shared `/libs` body: featured Genesis, library cards, and Community with QQ QR modal and Discord.
- **pages/RuntimeContent.astro** — `/runtime` hero followed by RuntimeExamples.
- **pages/RuntimeExamples.astro** — Tabbed code and live Plotly/Brax examples.

## Styling and Behavior
- Layout only mounts shared effects. `art/surface-glare.ts` owns the delegated pointer sheen for glass and solid surfaces, coalesces events into one frame and releases pending work through AnimationLifetime. Reduced transparency skips hidden glass sheen while keeping visible solid-surface lighting. AnimationLifetime disposal is idempotent; late cleanup registrations run immediately, and one failed cleanup does not prevent other resources releasing. Run `pnpm check:animation-lifetime` for its ownership checks.
- Glass filters retain typed references to their mutable SVG nodes. Resizing performs no selector queries and writes no diagnostic size attribute. Map acquisition/release is separate from filter updates; all layout reads complete before preset detachments or filter writes. All `.glass` roots are observed so switching either direction between frosted and refractive presets works after mounting; the frosted preset itself still creates no SVG filter.
- Browser checks share `scripts/lib/browser-checks.mjs` for local preview serving, baseline asset routing and checkout-local temporary directories. Missing current pages remain 404s; only missing hashed assets can fall back to the previous build. Use GLASS_VERIFY_IDENTICAL=1 with the rendering check when a refactor should preserve complete News/button pixels, including their rims. Package scripts expose `check:glass`, `check:glass:rendering` and `check:performance` without extra dependencies.
- Canvas components use `art/animation-lifetime.ts` to own event listeners and observers and cancel animation frames on normal document teardown, while preserving back/forward cache entries. The hero Canvas draws at up to 60fps, with terrain and pointer updates on the same cadence and next-landscape candidates prepared in roughly 1ms batches during stable frames. Other decorative Canvas scenes draw at about 30fps; hidden and offscreen scenes stop drawing. Inner-page Gaussian backgrounds reuse separable row/column samples. Run `node scripts/check-hero-smooth.mjs` after building to exercise automatic terrain shifts, mouse interaction, mobile layouts, both themes and reduced motion. Run `node scripts/check-home-performance.mjs` after building for browser checks; set `PERFORMANCE_SOAK_MS=90000` for GC-normalized heap/DOM sampling. Reports and screenshots go to `output/playwright/performance/`. `scripts/check-glass-lenses.mjs` compares both themes against a saved previous-build snapshot: News-centre pixels remain equivalent, while the refracted rim may change. It verifies the red primary button's transmission and readable white labels, the CSS-only rail, unchanged maps on theme changes, radial corners, the fixed 32px News bezel, visible folding masks, fractional nine-slice seams, constant-width optics on resize, zero image-URL writes through 12 header morph cycles, bounded resources, ordinary-glass fallbacks, reduced transparency and cleanup. GLASS_OUTPUT and GLASS_BASELINE select a report directory and previous build. `scripts/check-glass-rendering.mjs` additionally opens normal Chrome without its forced-sRGB automation flag, checks stripe direction on the actual display profile in both themes, saves matched-background before/after screenshots and compares frame cadence/main-thread work during header morphs. Its default output is `output/playwright/glass-v3/`; use GLASS_PERFORMANCE_ONLY=1 for an isolated timing run. Run timing checks without concurrent browser tests.
- Homepage diagrams share `--diagram-*` tokens in global.css: pale gray surfaces and white nodes in light mode, charcoal surfaces in dark mode, neutral structure and red activity. Canvas renderers refresh their palette and redraw on `data-theme` changes, including while paused or offscreen. `art/diagram-theme.ts` reads these tokens for the bundled renderers; the standalone inline evolution loop reads the same tokens directly.
- Shared glass materials live in `../styles/glass.css`, imported by global.css. Every surface uses the same `.glass` class and selects `data-glass="clear|accent|content|navigation|menu|frosted"`. Components own geometry and interaction only; do not scatter tint, blur, rim or fallback overrides into component styles. GitHub uses clear; Get Started uses accent; News uses content; Header and dropdowns use navigation/menu; the chapter rail uses frosted and is excluded from SVG refraction and pointer glare.
- The clear and accent control presets share their border, shadow, blur and optical model. Accent adds translucent red tint (42–56% in light mode, 64–76% in dark mode), a red-colored luminance transfer that preserves EvoX red and white-label contrast, and the same pointer sheen. It has no opaque core layer or extra blur. Content retains the original thin tint plus an 8% theme-background veil and 1px core blur. Its `--lg-edge-width: 32px` is independent of its 20px corner radius and of distortion strength. Other refractive surfaces use the regular width of 0.55 times their radius. Width and strength are separate preset controls. All surfaces share a base displacement rule (capped at scale 24) and 0.65px edge blur; `--lg-edge-strength` multiplies only the displacement scalar. News explicitly uses 2.2 (scale 48.4 at its 20px radius); controls and navigation remain at 1. Widening a rim alone never changes its strength.
- Layout mounts `art/glass-lenses.ts` on eligible fine-pointer Chromium desktops. It reads blur, bezel width and strength from the selected preset. `art/glass-optics.ts` adapts Nordify's measured bulge/compressed-lip profile (MIT notice included), normalized to the existing width/amplitude controls. The mask stays fully visible over the folding region and fades over the last 28% of the bezel. Small 96px tiles store X in grey and Y in alpha; a separate white/alpha tile set carries the mask. Decode to R/G directly before feDisplacementMap, with no intervening colour operations, to avoid Chrome Display-P3 cross-channel displacement. Transparent coordinate tiles must not be composited over a full-size neutral fill; only the nonoverlapping centre cell is filled. A responsive nine-slice map can extend beyond the corner radius using a rounded-box distance field; its inner normals blend smoothly. Each filter uses two feImage inputs and eight top-level primitives. Filters, map templates and tile sets are reference-counted, so unused geometry is released and resize history cannot accumulate. Closed/offscreen surfaces defer first initialization. All layout reads precede writes. Preset/theme changes and development CSS hot updates resynchronize optical values without changing the map geometry merely to adjust strength. Header layout variables define a responsive image-viewport radius expression, keeping image URLs unchanged through morphs. Document teardown releases maps, tiles, observers and frame callbacks through AnimationLifetime.
- Ordinary CSS glass remains the fallback for other browsers, touch devices and no JavaScript. The frosted preset always stays on that path. Presets also own denser no-backdrop fallbacks and opaque reduced-transparency fills; accent remains red with white text. GPU compositing still runs for each visible surface. Avoid nesting refractive glass, because a backdrop root prevents descendants from filtering the full page.
- Solid content cards use `.surface-card` and pill controls use `.surface-button` with `--surface-*` tokens, hover lift, pointer glow and a fine rim. Image labels use `.glass-chip`. Inner-page `.page-hero-backdrop` banners mount `art/contour-backdrop.ts`: four Gaussian peaks drift and breathe without agents or resets, using marching squares and height-ramped strokes shared with EvolutionField. The canvas draws at about 30fps, pauses hidden/offscreen and renders one static frame for reduced motion.
- `src/styles/global.css` owns `--site-*` colors and typography. Default dark uses black/charcoal surfaces with EvoX red; light uses white/pale-gray surfaces. Layout restores the saved theme before paint. Header and homepage wordmarks use theme-aware SVG lettering; Footer's existing word image uses the theme's logo filter. The X mark uses BrandMark's per-path CSS sweep and must never receive a whole-image filter.
- Instrument Sans is self-hosted in Latin and Latin Extended subsets; system fonts cover other scripts. Header and footer links are 12px; copyright is 11px. Shared chrome has a 1216px maximum inner width; the homepage body uses 1040px.
- Older content pages retain Tailwind utilities. `.site-main-padded` rules adapt surfaces, text, borders, prose, and hover states. Keep paired foreground/background states consistent when editing these rules.
- Homepage content remains visible without JavaScript. With JavaScript, the opening is a fixed overlay above the normal homepage and never reserves scrollable height. A single entry gesture starts the X flight into the header wordmark; completion removes the overlay and all temporary input guards. Ordinary scrolling and `#top` then reach the normal homepage without replaying the opening. Header fades in during entry, the target X appears at handoff while the EVO letters fade in with the navigation, and the chapter rail appears on completion. Other routes show navigation immediately. Reduced-motion preferences make entry instant and disable reveal motion and smooth scrolling.

## Constraints
- Use `loadTranslations(locale)` and `t()` for interface copy. Product names, social brand labels, and the public QQ group number are intentional literals.
- Use `getLocalizedUrl(path, locale)` for internal links without trailing slashes. External new-tab links require `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- Pages without a locale counterpart, such as 404, must pass `localePath` for valid language-menu destinations.
- Astro bundles typed component scripts; Footer's inline year script stays dependency-free. Icons use `astro-icon/components` with Heroicons or Simple Icons.

## Known Issues and Dependencies
- RuntimeContent retains `genesis.hero.github` for its GitHub link. Genesis is external; there is no internal Genesis route.
- RuntimeExamples depends on externally loaded Plotly and a lazy Brax iframe. Preserve readiness guards, resize calls, and fallback behavior.
- Header's legacy `animated` and `sticky` props are compatibility declarations only.
